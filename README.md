# 🏥 Se7ety — صحتي

### A Flutter medical app that connects patients with specialized doctors easily and securely.

## 📱 App Screens

### 🟦 Welcome & Onboarding
> Introduces the app to new users with a smooth onboarding experience.

- Search for specialized doctors across various medical fields
- Easy appointment booking anytime, anywhere
- Secure and private — your data is always protected

---

### 🔐 Authentication
> Simple and secure sign-up and login flow.

- Register with your basic info
- Login to your existing account
- OTP verification support

---

### 👤 Patient Screens
> Full-featured dashboard for patients.

- Browse and filter doctors by specialty
- View doctor profiles and ratings
- Book, manage, and track appointments
- In-app chat with your doctor
- Access your medical records and reports

---
